Back in June, the most loving woman i’ve ever met, Valerie, joined me in a trip through Croatia.

In the words I wrote back then: “I thought that pedaling through Europe would push me to the edge of emotional experience. Valerie brought me further. She has a character deep like the sea, and a heart of truth and passion.

Never to me was it so clear that wherever you go, the real journey is the one within.”
