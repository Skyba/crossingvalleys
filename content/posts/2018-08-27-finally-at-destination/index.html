It’s DONE! I Arrived at the border with Hong Kong! After 16 months and 4 days, 18077 kilometers and 24 countries…

It’s hard to describe how I feel right now. The end has suddenly come so fast. My entire soul is being carried away by melancholy. I wish this was not over, that I could live everything again.

I close my eyes. I try to remember all the landscapes I traversed, the people I met, all the emotions I have experienced. I finally have the complete picture of what this trip looks like. I remember my first nights outside 16 months ago – I feel like a different person now. The memories are inexhaustible and powerful. I almost can’t believe I’ve really done all this.

I don’t know where to start to share the lessons I learned along the way. More about it in coming posts. But right now a huge THANK YOU to the countless people who offered me their hospitality and help along the way. All those who believed in me and kept me going. Your support and messages made this trip happen. THANK YOU!!!
